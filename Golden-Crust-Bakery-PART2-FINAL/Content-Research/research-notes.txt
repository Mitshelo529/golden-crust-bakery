Golden Crust Bakery – Content Research Notes

The website content was planned for a small bakery in Johannesburg, South Africa.
Research focused on clear business information, product information, customer enquiries and contact details.

Content sources considered:
- Original business content
- Royalty-free bakery/product images
- Free icons
- Google Fonts
- Product photographs

External images and media should be checked for suitable licensing before use. Source and licensing information should be retained in the project research files and references.
